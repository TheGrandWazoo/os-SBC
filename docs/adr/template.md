# ADR-SBC-NNNN: <short title>

> **In short:** Two to four plain sentences: what we decided and the main
> reason. Someone who reads only this knows the outcome.

## Context

What problem are we facing, and what forces push on the decision? Write it
so someone who disagrees with the outcome still agrees this is the problem.

## Decision

One clear sentence, then the specifics needed to act on it.

## Alternatives considered

Each option, and why it lost.

## Consequences

What gets easier, what gets harder, and the follow-on work this creates.
Include the honest downsides.

## References

Links to sources, issues and related documents.
